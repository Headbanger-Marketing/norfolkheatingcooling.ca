About Us

Home Comfort, Rooted in Norfolk County

From the streets of Simcoe to the Lake Erie shoreline at Port Dover and Turkey Point, we keep Norfolk families comfortable through lake-effect winters and humid summer stretches.

Our Story

A Local HVAC Team That Knows Norfolk

Norfolk Heating & Cooling was built to serve the communities we actually live in, from Simcoe and Delhi down to the lakeshore at Port Rowan.

Norfolk County sits in a unique pocket of southwestern Ontario, and the climate here keeps your HVAC equipment working overtime. Cold air crossing Lake Erie drives heavy lake-effect snow each winter, while humid air off the water pushes air conditioners hard through July and August. Older homes in Waterford and Port Dover, alongside newer builds outside Simcoe, all have different comfort demands, and we've spent years learning what each one needs.

As a family-run company based right on Alice Street in Simcoe, we treat every furnace repair, ductless installation, and duct cleaning like we're working on a neighbour's home, because most of the time we are. Our technicians live locally, drive the same county roads you do, and understand that a failed furnace in January or an AC breakdown during a heat wave isn't an inconvenience, it's an urgent problem for your family.

That local focus is why homeowners across Norfolk rely on us for honest assessments, fair pricing, and work that holds up through every season. We're proud to be the heating and cooling team families from Delhi to Turkey Point call first.
